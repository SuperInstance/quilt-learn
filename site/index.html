L2 · the explorer games

Entropy is not a method

Four minds, one bandit, paired noise, ten seeds. UCB1's optimism is a formula cell; BOLTZ and QUANTUM share the same annealed-softmax formulas and differ in exactly one thing — where their randomness comes from. The quantum mind's bits were harvested from a real MOTHquantum measurement (one budgeted job), digest-seeded and receipted.

Finding: the quantum explorer lost. True randomness bought nothing that policy structure (UCB1's bound) didn't buy cheaper. Entropy is a budget to ration, not a strategy.

L3 · the optimizer breeder

The twin nominates; the sheet elects

A GAN whose generator proposes update rules and whose discriminator is the learning problem itself — judged at championship distance with quilt-native gesture metrics (arc length, bending energy) on the loss curve. The GAN breeds on a vectorized twin proven equivalent to the sheet at 1.67e-16; the crowned genome then defends its title on the living engine.

Finding: the bred crown took runner-up (2.95e-2) behind the hand-tuned classic momentum(0.3) (9.5e-6). The championship also caught momentum(0.06) converging to 7e-3 and then exploding — the exact instability the discriminator's bending-energy term punishes.
